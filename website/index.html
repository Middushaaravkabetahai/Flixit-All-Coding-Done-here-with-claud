When does Flixit launch?

October 20, 2026, on the App Store and Google Play, at the same time.

How does the closet scan work?

You take one photo of your closet or a rack of clothes, and Flixit identifies the individual items in it. You review what it found and fix the category, color, or brand on anything it got wrong before it saves to your wardrobe.

Do I have to add every item by hand?

No. Scanning your closet adds several pieces at once. You can still add items one at a time when you want full control, and you can delete anything later.

Who can see my wardrobe photos?

Only you. Wardrobe photos are stored privately against your own account, and Flixit doesn't make them visible to other users.

What will it cost?

Pricing hasn't been finalized. We'll announce it closer to launch.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Flixit is built as a single app for both, so it launches on iOS and Android at the same time.
