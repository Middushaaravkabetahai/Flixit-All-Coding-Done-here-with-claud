FLIXIT

Your closet. Your style.
Your next move.

Flixit turns the clothes you already own into daily outfits, and helps you find better prices on everything else — one scan of your closet at a time.

01

Scan your closet

Photograph your closet once and Flixit picks out each item, so you're not adding clothes one at a time.

02

Plan your outfits

Daily outfit recommendations built from your wardrobe, the occasion, and color coordination. Don't like it? Switch it up.

03

Find the best price

Scan something in a store and compare it against prices online before you pay full price for it.

04

Swipe on Flixnder

Swipe right on the pieces you like. Every swipe teaches Flixit what your style actually is.

05

Your Style FYP

A feed that reorders itself around what you scan, swipe and save — not what someone paid to put in front of you.
